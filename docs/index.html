How the odds work

Updated hourly from ESPN rosters, projections and scores. We simulate the remaining season and four-team playoffs 20,000 times to estimate each team's chances. Earlier dashed points are reconstructed estimates, not predictions recorded at the time; historical runs use fewer simulations.
