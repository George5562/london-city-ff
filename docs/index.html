How the odds work

Championship probabilities are estimated by a 20,000-realisation hierarchical Monte Carlo ensemble. Position-constrained roster selection and availability-adjusted projections establish each team’s scoring baseline, normalised to the league’s empirical scoring environment. An eight-game projection prior regularises observed performance: μᵢ = (8pᵢ + PFᵢ)/(8 + nᵢ).

Each realisation samples latent team strength with Gaussian dispersion σ = 7, then propagates fixture-level scoring uncertainty with σ = 26. In-progress fixtures condition on banked points and residual projected production, with residual variance attenuated by the unplayed scoring fraction. Simulated standings are ordered by wins and points-for before a four-team knockout recursion; title probability is the empirical expectation of the championship indicator, P̂ᵢ = N⁻¹∑ₛ 𝟙{championₛ = i}.
