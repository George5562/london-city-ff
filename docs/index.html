How the odds work

Every day we pull each roster from ESPN, pick every team's best possible lineup using ESPN's player projections, and blend that with how many points the team has actually scored so far. Then we simulate the rest of the regular season and the 4-team playoff 20,000 times. A team's title odds are the share of those simulations it wins.
